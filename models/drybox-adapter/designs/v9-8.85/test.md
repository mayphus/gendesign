# Physical test — v9 pocket

- **Printed?** Yes. The recorded result is that printed holes come out about 0.1–0.2 mm undersize. No date was written down.
- **Settings:** Not recorded. Print this small part at 100% infill, no supports, with the 45° cone able to print cleanly.
- **Measured vs designed:** One insert measured about 8.7 mm across the lands that enter the pocket. A pocket drawn at that size will not accept it, which is why v9 is drawn at 8.85 mm. The printed pocket was not logged as one caliper number, only that 0.1–0.2 mm band.
- **Fit / function:** Too tight when the drawn pocket equals the pin. On one dryer the insert went straight into the wall hole, so this adapter may be unnecessary — check that before printing a set.
- **Photos:** None saved. `docs/v9_section_compare.png` is a CAD section.
- **Failure notes:** Do not pick a pocket from CAD volume. Test the real insert.
- **Next change:** If a coupon will not start, raise P by 0.05–0.10 mm and reprint. If it rattles, go down. Write the printed diameter into the next `test.md`.
