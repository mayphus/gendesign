## Checks

## Concepts

- **pocket sweep** (chosen): One gene (P). A printed coupon settles it faster than any model of the hole.
- **topology optimization** (rejected): Nothing load-bearing to optimize: the insert sits in a pocket; the risk is tolerance.

Chosen: **pocket sweep**, generated by `pocket`.
