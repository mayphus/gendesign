Requirements → design targets → concept. Written by `gendesign analyze` on 2026-11-15; no geometry was drawn to get here.
Rounds read these targets. Re-run after requirements.yaml or a calibration changes.

## Design targets

| Target | Value | From | How |
|---|---:|---|---|
| tip_load_N | 50.0 | function.tip_load_N | stated design load at the right edge |
| volfrac_max | 0.4 | constraints.volfrac_max | SIMP volume upper bound; gene volfrac searches under this |
| min_feature_mm | 2.0 | constraints.rmin_mm_min | density-filter radius sets minimum member width |
| domain_mm | [120.0, 40.0, 5.0] | fit.length/height/thickness_mm | rectangular design space around two left bolt holes |

## Checks

- Load 50 N binds the layout; volume ≤ 40% trades mass against stiffness.
- Steel E=200.0 GPa, σ_y≈250.0 MPa (yield not constrained in this round).
- Print flat (thickness = build Z): in-plane overhang filter off.

## Concepts

- **2-D SIMP topology** (chosen): Free material layout inside the rectangle under the bolt BCs and tip load; genes are volfrac, filter radius, and load height.
- **parametric two-bar truss** (rejected): Assumes the load path shape; cannot discover bolt pads or branching members.

Chosen: **2-D SIMP topology**, generated by `topopt-bracket`.

## Open questions for the owner

- Tip load 50 N is a stated design load, not a measured service load.
- Yield 250 MPa is reference only; the round does not constrain stress.
